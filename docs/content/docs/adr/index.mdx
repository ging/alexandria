# Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

A record captures a decision that was expensive to make and would be expensive to reverse: its context, the decision, its consequences and what would change it.

<Callout type="info">
Records are never rewritten once accepted. A decision that no longer holds is superseded by a later record and keeps its original text.
</Callout>
