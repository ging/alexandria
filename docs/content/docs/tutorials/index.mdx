# Hands-On Tutorials — The GreenCity Mobility Story

To make every piece click together effortlessly, all five tutorials follow **one single dataspace from start to finish**: **GreenCity Mobility (`mobility`)**, a municipal bike and e-scooter authority sharing real-time fleet telemetry with partner apps.

Meet our cast:
- **`ana` (`vhub-admin`)** — Hub administrator who bootstraps the `mobility` namespace and appoints its policy steward.
- **`sergio` (Namespace Steward)** — Domain steward who registers vocabularies, authors policy profiles, validates `N1`–`N5` rules, and publishes releases (`1.0.0` → `2.0.0`).
- **`alexandria-client`** — The edge validation sidecar guarding GreenCity's DSP connector, syncing signed packages and validating real `ContractRequestMessage` payloads locally.

Every flow includes **copy-pastable YAML/CLI commands** alongside **recorded Console walkthrough videos** so you can learn whichever way feels most natural.
